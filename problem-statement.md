Problem Statement – Bharat Sahayak AI

Introduction

India provides various government welfare schemes to support farmers, students, women, and economically weaker sections. However, many citizens are unaware of these schemes.

Existing Problems

1. Lack of awareness about government welfare schemes.
2. Difficulty finding suitable schemes.
3. Language barriers while understanding information.
4. Confusion about eligibility criteria.
5. Lack of knowledge about required documents.
6. Difficulty understanding application procedures.

Target Users

- Farmers
- Students
- Women
- Rural citizens
- Economically weaker families

Impact

Many eligible citizens may miss opportunities to receive government benefits because they cannot easily access or understand scheme information.

Need for the Project

A simple, accessible, multilingual platform is needed to help citizens discover government schemes and understand the basic application process.
