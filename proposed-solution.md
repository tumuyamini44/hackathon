Proposed Solution – Bharat Sahayak AI

Introduction

Bharat Sahayak AI is an AI-powered government scheme discovery and assistance platform designed to make government welfare information accessible to every citizen.

Our Solution

The platform helps users discover suitable government schemes through a simple interface with Telugu and English language support.

Key Features

1. Scheme Discovery: Find government schemes based on user needs.
2. Language Support: Understand information in Telugu and English.
3. Eligibility Guidance: Get preliminary guidance about scheme eligibility.
4. Document Information: View documents commonly required for applications.
5. Application Guidance: Understand the basic application process.
6. Official Links: Visit government websites for verified information.

How It Works

1. User opens Bharat Sahayak AI.
2. User searches for a government scheme.
3. Platform displays relevant scheme information.
4. User explores benefits and required documents.
5. User checks preliminary eligibility guidance.
6. User visits the official government portal for further verification and application.

Expected Outcome

- Improved awareness of government schemes.
- Reduced language barriers.
- Easier access to scheme information.
- Better digital accessibility for rural citizens.

Conclusion

Bharat Sahayak AI aims to make government scheme information simple, accessible, and understandable for everyone.
